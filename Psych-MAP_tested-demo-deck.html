The questions behind the data

“Has the patient been sleeping?”

Room presence can show where a tag was detected. It cannot establish sleep quality.

Psych-MAP connects the question to relevant records and tells staff what is still unknown.

The same distinction applies to eating, medication-taking, mood and symptoms.
